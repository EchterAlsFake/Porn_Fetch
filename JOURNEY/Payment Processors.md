# The Payment Processor mess 
When I wanted to start commercializing Porn Fetch the second biggest problem, after
the legal trouble were the payment processors.

# The Monopoly
If you want to process cards via Visa / Mastercard you need to follow their strict 
guidelines and Acceptable Use Policies. 

If you didn't know, Stripe, Paypal and all the other services that do this for you 
by providing you with a nice interface, an API and so on also need to follow those strict
guidelines.

You can't just take a card as an individual person and take money from it. It 
requires strong legal compliance to guarantee the security, enforce Anti Money Laundering laws
and so on. That is the reason why you must rely on a so called "Payment Processor", which
basically takes the leaal headache away from you and typically takes a small share from
the transaction.

This is the de-facto standard on the Internet and also the Offline World.


# The High-Risk Problem
There are certain industries where this does not work out. E.g., the Adult Industry,
CBD, Gambling etc. For those industries, you require a special setup and merchant code 
for Visa and Mastercard, because those industries have a higher risk of chargebacks.
Additionally, the underlying banks that actually process the money don't really like
such industries due to their own moral and other factors.

So, as a payment processor you need to have a special bound to banks which allow that,
and you need to close special deals with Visa and Mastercard. Each merchant must 
additionally pay yearly fees (around 1.450€).

Those High-Risk Payment processors also have much stricter requirements. They usually take
10-15%+ of each transaction which is huge. They also commonly enforce minimum thresholds
which are way too high for my business, as I don't know yet, how much Porn Fetch
will actually make. However, even worse, they also keep your money for some months
for "Security" and anti-fraud reasons. This would literally destroy my business and
ruin me. 

I have searched ALL High-Risk Payment processors as well as regional payment processors.
There is NONE that fits my specific needs and I have therefore no way to process a 
credit card / Google Pay / Apple Pay or whatever.

# MoR 
There are platforms like Patreon, Lemon Squeezy and so on which act as a Merchant of Record.
Those platforms actually buy the product from you, and then sell it to your customer.
They therefore automatically handle Tax for you and also take some legal headaches from you
and act as a small shield. This would be perfect for Porn Fetch, but all of those MoRs explicitly
forbid the Adult Industry.

# But Porn Fetch doesn't actually show Porn? 
Yes, that's true. My Website as well as the Software does not show any NSFW material.
However, that doesn't matter, because those Acceptable Use Policies don't only forbid the NSFW
part but also services that interact with the adult industry.

### Other Problems
Those Payment processors also usually don't like:

While Porn Fetch is fully legal in Germany, it is not in the US. 
Most of these processors are based in the US, so I can't get around this.

# NexaPay
NexaPay is a platform that allows you to buy a product with Card / Apple Pay
and everything else, and send it to me in crypto. They don't require any Know Your Customer
or Know Your Business, however there's a problem with that.

They are by German Law illegal and don't have a valid EU license. Therefore using this
platform would be an insane high risk gambble, although it would fix all my problems instantly lol.


# Bank to Bank
Thanks to EU laws banks must open up their payment infrastructure for others. There are
services like Wallid.co which basically do this automated and for all EU banks.

So, you would click on Buy, and Wallid would redirect you into your Banking App.
You then authorize the transaction and the money is sent directly to my bank account.

However, this only works in the EU, so all customers from Asia / US and so on
would be excluded from this, and there's a startup fee of 99€ for High Risk business.
So, this isn't worth it for now, but an option I consider for later.

# Crypto (The Solution)
Thanks, to a Platform called NowPayments.io I can actually do all this perfectly
fine using Crypto Payments. They accept over 350+ currencies and convert them directly into
a stable coin. That way there is no bank involved, no Visa / Mastercard. Nobody that can censor me,
which is perfect. 

Problem? 
You need to understand how to buy crypto and how to use it. 
Most people don't which is the reason why this is not perfect.


### The Fiat Solution
Services like Transak let you buy Crypto directly with your Credit Card.
As a merchant you can create a link which will send the coins directly to a specific wallet
address. So I create the NowPayments payment, you buy the crypto using your credit
card and the money is sent to me. 

This is much safer, because technically you are not buying my restricted product.
You are buying crypto and sending it to me. 


However, this still creates user friction, because you need to wait for the payment to suceed
which can take a few minutes and it's not as easy and safe as a direct payment.
