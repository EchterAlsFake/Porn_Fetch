# Country-specific restrictions

Indonesia: Articles 4, 5 and 31 of Law No. 44/2008 prohibit downloading certain pornographic materials. The adult-video downloading functionality is not intended for use in Indonesia.

Bangladesh: Section 4 of the Pornography Control Act 2012 prohibits, among other activities, storing and transporting pornography. Downloading and retaining adult videos may violate this law.

United Arab Emirates: Local telecommunications rules restrict access to pornographic content and services designed to bypass mandatory ISP content filtering. The application's privacy and proxy features must not be used to circumvent these restrictions.

Saudi Arabia: Article 6 of the Anti-Cyber Crime Law prohibits certain activities involving material contrary to public morals, including covered storage activities. Downloading and storing pornographic content may violate these provisions.

Iran: Articles 14 and 15 of the Computer Crimes Act restrict certain activities involving pornography, including facilitating access to pornographic content. Unauthorized internet-filtering circumvention tools are also restricted.

Oman: Article 48 of the Cybercrime Law issued under Royal Decree 61/2026 prohibits specified activities involving the creation, transmission and publication of pornographic materials. Whether private downloading is covered requires separate assessment.

Belarus: Access to and use of Tor and other internet anonymization tools are subject to government restrictions.

China: Unapproved cross-border VPN and telecommunications services are restricted. The legality of individual proxy and Tor configurations depends on their operation and applicable regulations.

Turkmenistan: VPN and circumvention technologies face extensive blocking and enforcement measures. Users may face legal or administrative consequences.

Network Privacy

The application supports DNS over HTTPS, optional proxy-based traffic handling and Tor routing.

These features provide different levels of network privacy and do not guarantee complete anonymity. Enabling them does not make otherwise prohibited activity lawful.

Copyright and Content

The application does not grant permission to reproduce content from third-party websites. Downloads must be authorized by the relevant rights holder or permitted under applicable law.

The software must not be used to obtain prohibited content, violate copyright, or access computer systems without authorization.
