# Shipped application graphics

The six SVG navigation icons in `src/frontend/graphics/` (`account`,
`information`, `settings`, `download`, `database`, and `progressbars`) were
redrawn specifically for Porn Fetch in September 2026. They contain no copied
IconScout artwork. They are covered by the application license.

The PNG logo, its transparent PNG/ICO/ICNS variants, and `splashscreen.png`
remain in the repository. `docs/CREDITS.md` identifies the logo as generated
with ChatGPT, but no original generation record or independent rights evidence
is stored here. Confirm their provenance and any embedded inputs before a
source-available release. The four screenshots under `src/frontend/screenshots/`
are historical project screenshots and are not embedded by `resources.qrc`.

Do not restore former IconScout SVGs without checking the exact license granted
when each file was acquired. Current IconScout terms limit redistribution of
source assets and certain adult uses; credits alone do not resolve those terms.
